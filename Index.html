Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about how we run and scale your advertising accounts.

How fast can we see results after launching ad campaigns? +
For paid channels like Meta and Google Ads, initial test data starts coming in within 48 to 72 hours. We run aggressive creative testing in the first 7 days to identify winning hooks and begin scaling ROAS in weeks 2-3.
Do you manage our ad spend directly or do we pay the ad platform? +
You always retain 100% ownership of your ad accounts. You pay the ad platforms (Meta/Google) directly via your credit card or billing method. We charge a transparent agency management fee based on spend tier or flat retainer.
How do you handle iOS tracking issues and conversion drop-offs? +
We implement full Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and Google Tag Manager server-side tracking with complete event deduplication. This recovers 25-35% of lost tracking signals caused by iOS updates.
What is included in your free initial marketing audit? +
Our free audit analyzes your current pixel setup, wasted search terms in Google Ads, creative fatigue scores on Meta, and an organic technical SEO health score with immediate fixes.
